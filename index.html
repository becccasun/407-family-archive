No. 02 Process

How it works

Four steps. You do the first two at your kitchen table.

  1. 01

    Order your kit

    Pick a size based on roughly how much you have. It ships in 2–3 days.

  2. 02

    Pack your memories

    Sleeve the prints, box the letters, fill out the inventory sheet. No sorting perfection needed.

  3. 03

    We scan & restore

    Send it in the prepaid, insured box. We scan everything and fix dust, scratches, and fading.

  4. 04

    Receive your archive

    You get an online gallery, a USB drive, and your originals back, filed and labeled.
